Privacy

Shell Run · last updated October 2026

What we store

Your progress (levels, stars, shells, skins, settings) is saved on your device only. We do not create accounts and we do not collect your name, email or location.

Gameplay events

The game keeps a short local log of gameplay events (for example "level started" or "level finished") so we can improve difficulty. In the web version this log never leaves your device.

Ads and purchases

The web version shows demo ads and a demo store with no real charges. When Shell Run launches in the app stores, ads and purchases will be handled by the store and by our ad partners, and this page will list them and what they collect, with a way to opt out where the law gives you one.

Children

Shell Run is designed to be safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Moving your progress

Settings → Move my progress creates a save code. The code contains only your game progress. Anyone you share it with can load your progress.

Contact

Questions? Use the contact link on the store page.
